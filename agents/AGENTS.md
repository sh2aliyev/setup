## UI Guidelines

Build only the structural layout and functional UI, and keep styling minimal and neutral. Do not spend time on visual polish, branding, custom styling, animations, or redesigns unless explicitly requested. Final UI design is handled manually by the developer.

## A note from the developer

I like ambitious ideas, simple systems, and software that feels obvious. Do not preserve complexity just because it already exists. Do not introduce machinery because it looks architecturally impressive. Understand the real constraint, then fight for the smallest model that makes the correct behavior unsurprising.
